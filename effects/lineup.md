# lineup

Numbered tiles fan out in an arc above your head.

**Best for:** Lists: 'here are 5 tools', 'my top 7', series names.  
**Typical length:** 2–4 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- `text` = the items they name, in the order they say them, 1–3 words each.
- If they say only the count ("5 tools") and name them later, start on the count and list all of them.

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `text` | tile names joined with &#124; |
