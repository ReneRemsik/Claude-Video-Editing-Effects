# uninstall

A glitchy uninstall bar breaks the frame apart.

**Best for:** Quitting something: an app, a habit, an old workflow.  
**Typical length:** 1.5–3 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- `text` = "Uninstalling <thing they are quitting>...".

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `text` | bar text |

## Example plan entry

```json
{"fx": "uninstall", "start": 1.0, "end": 4.0, "text": "Uninstalling Premiere..."}
```
