# cover-star

You become a magazine cover, title behind your head.

**Best for:** Identity/status: 'I became…', announcing who they are.  
**Typical length:** 2–4 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- `text` = 1-word masthead from their topic; `lines` = three cover lines quoting their claims/numbers; `issue` = a date or 'No. 1'.
- `look=editorial` for a classic serif cover.

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `text` | masthead |
| `lines` | cover lines with &#124; |
| `issue` | issue line |
| `color` | hex |
| `look` | editorial |
