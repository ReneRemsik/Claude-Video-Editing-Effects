# profile-wall

Your reels grid fills the wall behind you while a counter ticks up.

**Best for:** Growth stories, 'I posted every day'.  
**Typical length:** 3–5 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- `count` = number of posts/videos they mention; `label` = what the counter counts; `name`/`sub` from the user.
