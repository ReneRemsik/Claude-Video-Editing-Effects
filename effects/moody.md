# moody

A moody colour grade.

**Best for:** Serious, reflective or storytelling passages.  
**Typical length:** any s

## Fill it from the transcript

- Apply over the reflective section ("when I was…", "honestly"). Use `bw` for flashbacks, `warm` for nostalgic/positive, `cool` for tech.

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `grade` | moody, bw, warm, cool, pop |

## Example plan entry

```json
{"fx": "moody", "start": 1.0, "end": 5.0, "grade": "moody"}
```
