# headline-box

A punch-in with a two-line headline, the second line in a white box.

**Best for:** Hooks and thesis lines.  
**Typical length:** 2–3 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- `text` = setup|payoff split of their hook sentence.

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `text` | 2 lines with &#124; |
| `zoom` |  |
