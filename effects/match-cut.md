# match-cut

News pages cut on one word, locked in a red box behind your head.

**Best for:** 'Everyone is talking about X'.  
**Typical length:** 2–3 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- `text` = the topic word they say (a product, person, trend).

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `text` | word |
| `color` | hex |

## Example plan entry

```json
{"fx": "match-cut", "start": 1.0, "end": 4.0, "text": "Claude"}
```
