# swipe

A before/after slider sweeps across you.

**Best for:** Transformations: 'before', 'after', 'used to… now'.  
**Typical length:** 1.5–3 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- Start on "before"/"after" or the contrast word; pick `grade` for the after look.

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `grade` | the after look |

```json
{"fx": "swipe", "start": 1.0, "end": 4.0, "grade": "pop"}
```
