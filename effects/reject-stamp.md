# reject-stamp

A big red stamp slams onto the screen.

**Best for:** Myths, bad advice, 'no', 'stop doing this'.  
**Typical length:** 1–2 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- Start on the word of rejection; `text` = 1–2 words (REJECTED, MYTH, WRONG, NOPE).

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `text` | stamp text |
| `color` | hex |
| `angle` | deg |
| `y` | 0–1 |

## Example plan entry

```json
{"fx": "reject-stamp", "start": 1.0, "end": 3.0, "text": "MYTH"}
```
