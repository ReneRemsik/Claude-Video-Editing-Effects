# noise

Reels pile up behind your head, one every quarter second.

**Best for:** Hooks about overwhelm, too much content, 'everyone is posting'.  
**Typical length:** 1.5–3 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- Start on the word that names the overload ("so many", "everyone", "noise").
- If the user has screenshots or clips of the thing they talk about, pass them as `images`; otherwise frames of their own video are used.

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `images` | folder of screenshots or clips |
| `size` | card width, share of frame (0.34) |

## Example plan entry

```json
{"fx": "noise", "start": 1.0, "end": 4.0}
```
