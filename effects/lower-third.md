# lower-third

A glass name tag slides in at the bottom.

**Best for:** Introductions: 'I'm X and I…'.  
**Typical length:** 2.5–4 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- `name` = their name, `title` = how they describe themselves (from the transcript) or their role. Start on "I'm…".
