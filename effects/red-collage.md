# red-collage

Black-and-white you, a giant red word behind, three lines slam in.

**Best for:** Bold statements, manifestos.  
**Typical length:** 2–4 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- `text` = the one-word thesis; `lines` = three short punchy phrases from the transcript; `tag` = handle or topic.

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `text` | red word |
| `lines` | 3 lines with &#124; |
| `tag` | small tag |
| `color` | hex |

## Example plan entry

```json
{"fx": "red-collage", "start": 1.0, "end": 5.0, "text": "EDIT", "lines": "no timeline|no apps|just words"}
```
