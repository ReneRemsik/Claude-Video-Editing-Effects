# scribble

Marker doodles draw themselves around you.

**Best for:** Playful emphasis, pointing at yourself.  
**Typical length:** 1.5–3 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- `text` = short handwritten label from their words.

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `text` | label |
| `color` | hex |

## Example plan entry

```json
{"fx": "scribble", "start": 1.0, "end": 4.0, "text": "me!"}
```
