# orbit

Your reels circle your head: in front at the bottom, behind at the top.

**Best for:** Talking about a body of work, a content system, 'all my videos'.  
**Typical length:** 2–4 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- Start when they mention their own content/portfolio.
- Use `images` if the user supplied thumbnails of the videos they mention.

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `images` | folder of images/clips |
| `count` | cards (6) |
| `speed` | spin speed (1.9) |
| `size` | card width (0.2) |

## Example plan entry

```json
{"fx": "orbit", "start": 1.0, "end": 5.0}
```
