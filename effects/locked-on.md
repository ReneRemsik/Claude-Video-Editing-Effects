# locked-on

Corner brackets snap onto your face and the camera follows you.

**Best for:** 'Focus', 'look at me', calling out themselves, a reveal.  
**Typical length:** 1.5–3 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- Start on the word that turns attention to the speaker ("me", "focus", "watch").
- `text` = a 1–2 word tag that names them or the moment ("TARGET ACQUIRED", their role).

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `zoom` | camera zoom (1.35) |
| `text` | tag under the brackets |

## Example plan entry

```json
{"fx": "locked-on", "start": 1.0, "end": 4.0, "text": "THE EDITOR"}
```
