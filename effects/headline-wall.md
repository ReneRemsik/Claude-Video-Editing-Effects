# headline-wall

Your own headlines pile up behind your head, then a total pops in front.

**Best for:** Social proof: 'my videos got X views'.  
**Typical length:** 3–5 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- `text` = titles they mention, each `headline::meta`; `total` = the total they say; `label` = what it counts; `handle` from the user.
