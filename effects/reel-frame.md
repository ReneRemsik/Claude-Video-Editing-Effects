# reel-frame

Your video shrinks into an Instagram reel card.

**Best for:** Meta moments: 'this reel', 'when you post this'.  
**Typical length:** 2–3 s

## Fill it from the transcript

- `text` = the user's handle.

## Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `text` | handle |

## Example plan entry

```json
{"fx": "reel-frame", "start": 1.0, "end": 4.0, "text": "@yourhandle"}
```
